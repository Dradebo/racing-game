# Striving Observation — Observation Doctrine

## Striving is broader than artifact production

Striving Observation must not mistake visible artifacts for the whole of striving.

Commits, documents, deployments, emails, tickets, reports, screenshots and releases are strong evidence because they leave durable traces. They are not the only evidence that a race has moved.

Meaningful striving can occur when no new artifact is produced.

Examples include:

- brainstorming that materially changes the shape of a project;
- research that narrows the viable solution space;
- discovering that an assumed implementation path is wrong;
- clarifying a finish line;
- redefining a circuit;
- recognizing a blocker or dependency;
- deciding that a race should be parked;
- deciding that an old race has been superseded;
- resolving ambiguity through conversation;
- choosing between competing approaches;
- recognizing that work thought to be large is actually small;
- recognizing that work thought to be nearly done has a consequential missing step;
- planning a transition or handoff;
- testing an idea mentally or conversationally and rejecting it before code is written;
- learning enough from research that the next legal lap changes;
- deliberately resting while maintaining a clear resume point.

The mirror should therefore observe **state-changing striving**, not merely file creation.

## Evidence can be conversational and interpretive

A conversation, research session or design discussion may produce a canonical state transition when it changes one or more of the following:

- finish line;
- current circuit;
- current lap;
- lap legality;
- dependency state;
- blocker state;
- waiting ownership;
- confidence level;
- race status;
- successor relationship;
- abandonment/parking decision;
- resume pointer;
- next legal lap.

Such evidence should be represented with appropriate provenance and confidence.

The rule remains:

> Event ≠ progress. Verified state transition = progress.

A long brainstorming session is not automatically progress.

But if that session resolves the architecture, invalidates a path, narrows the scope, changes the finish line, or makes the next legal lap obvious, then the race state has changed even if no commit followed immediately.

## Observe broadly, adjudicate narrowly

The system should ingest broadly from available evidence:

- repositories;
- documents;
- chat and brainstorming;
- research notes;
- email;
- calendar and meetings where relevant;
- deployments;
- manual declarations;
- agent outputs;
- external evidence;
- periods of inactivity or deliberate rest when they materially explain race state.

But the reducer should remain conservative.

Broad observation does not mean broad interpretation.

The mirror should ask:

1. Did something about the race actually change?
2. What changed?
3. What evidence supports that claim?
4. How confident are we?
5. Does this affect the current lap, circuit, status, dependency, finish line or resume pointer?

If the answer is unclear, preserve the evidence without forcing a state transition.

## This is not the synthesis engine

Striving Observation is a mirror.

It is not responsible for discovering deep thematic parallels, cross-domain synergies, hidden capability clusters, commercial opportunities, conceptual genealogies or broader meaning across projects.

Those functions may be handled by separate tools.

The mirror should not become a recommendation engine merely because it has a rich view of the corpus.

Its job is narrower and more demanding:

> Show the state of striving as faithfully as possible.

It may preserve relationships that are already evidenced, such as successor, dependency, mutation or explicit shared lineage.

It should not invent higher-order parallels simply because two projects look similar.

## Every ounce of striving can matter

The system should be capable of accounting for effort that leaves different kinds of traces:

### Concrete execution

Code, documents, deployments, reports, calls, releases, edits, field activity and shipped artifacts.

### Cognitive execution

Research, reasoning, design decisions, architecture, scoping, diagnosis, comparison, rejection of bad paths and clarification of ambiguity.

### Coordination

Handoffs, requests, waiting states, follow-ups, approvals, dependencies and external ownership.

### Recovery

Returning to a stalled race, reconstructing context, recovering lost work, re-establishing a resume pointer or resolving a broken transition.

### Deliberate non-action

Parking, waiting, resting, abandoning, deferring or refusing work when those are intentional state decisions rather than missing information.

The goal is not to award points for all activity.

The goal is to avoid losing meaningful state simply because it did not produce a conventional artifact.

## Artifact production is not the ontology

Artifacts are evidence-bearing game objects, but they are not the ontology itself.

The ontology is the striving state:

Season → Race → Circuit → Lap → Split → Baton

Artifacts, conversations, research, decisions and observations can all contribute evidence toward transitions inside that structure.

This distinction matters because otherwise the system will systematically underestimate:

- research-heavy work;
- architecture work;
- planning;
- diagnosis;
- decision-making;
- recovery;
- scope refinement;
- work done in conversation before implementation.

## The mirror must preserve silence correctly

No new commit does not mean no movement.

No new document does not mean no movement.

No visible movement also does not automatically mean hidden progress.

Silence must be interpreted through evidence.

Possible states include:

- active cognitive progress;
- waiting external;
- deliberate rest;
- blocked;
- parked;
- stale/no signal;
- abandoned;
- completed but not yet formally closed;
- unresolved.

The mirror should distinguish these rather than collapsing them into inactivity.

## Separation of concerns

Striving Observation owns:

- state custody;
- race reconstruction;
- provenance;
- confidence;
- obligations;
- waiting ownership;
- blockers;
- current and next legal laps;
- historical replay state;
- resume pointers;
- truthful orientation.

Other tools may own:

- cross-project synthesis;
- finding parallels and synergies;
- opportunity discovery;
- commercial interpretation;
- capability clustering;
- recommendation generation;
- strategic portfolio analysis.

This separation protects the mirror from becoming noisy or manipulative.

## Final doctrine

Observe broadly.

Reduce conservatively.

Preserve provenance.

Do not confuse artifacts with striving.

Do not confuse silence with inactivity.

Do not confuse observation with synthesis.

Every meaningful state-changing ounce of striving may enter the mirror.

The mirror's only obligation is to show what is there, as truthfully as the evidence permits.
